# Reliant India — Product Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Concierge MVP

Public marketplace + teacher profiles + student requirements + checkout UI + manual operations.

The goal is to prove that people will pay, not to automate everything immediately.

## Phase 1 — Accounts

Parent account:
- Saved students
- Requirements
- Bookings
- Payment history
- Invoices
- Reviews

Teacher account:
- Profile
- Verification
- Membership
- Student leads
- Accepted students
- Availability
- Earnings
- Payout status

## Phase 2 — Managed operations

- Automated notifications
- Attendance
- Session notes
- Replacement requests
- Refund states
- Support tickets
- Admin approval queues
- Payout ledger

## Phase 3 — Marketplace mechanics

- Search filters
- Matching score
- Lead credits
- Teacher subscriptions
- Featured profile placements
- Reviews
- Referral program
- Teacher performance analytics

## Phase 4 — Education platform

- Assessments
- Study plans
- Progress dashboards
- Learning resources
- Video classes
- Homework and assignments
- Parent reports

## Phase 5 — Scale

- City expansion
- Subject expansion
- School partnerships
- Corporate/school B2B programs
- Mobile applications
- Automated lifecycle marketing
